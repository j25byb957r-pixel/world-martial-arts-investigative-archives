Pangainoon

A focused portal for the traditional hard/soft explanation, its historical use, and the unresolved question of its exact Chinese provenance.

Evidence status: traditional identification preserved; formal historical provenance remains open.

Traditional explanation

The name is commonly explained within Uechi tradition as expressing a half-hard/half-soft relationship.

Historical problem

The archive separates the later explanation from the harder question of when the term entered the written record and whether an independently documented Chinese school used the exact designation.

Comparative value

Hard/soft complementarity remains useful for comparison, but it does not establish a unique lineage on its own.
