Seisan

A focused portal for Seisan as one of the three forms repeatedly associated with Kanbun Uechi's earlier curriculum.

Historical position

Within Uechi historical material, Seisan belongs to the three-form core preserved alongside Sanchin and Sanseiryu.

Comparative use

The archive uses Seisan as a reference point when comparing older transmitted material with later Okinawan curriculum development, without assuming that forms sharing a name are identical in origin.

Video comparison

Curated demonstrations can be added here by lineage and date. Videos illustrate how identified traditions present Seisan; they do not establish genealogy by themselves.
