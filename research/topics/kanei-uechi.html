Kanei Uechi

A focused portal for the postwar development and organization of Uechi-Ryū under Kanbun Uechi's son, separating later systematization from the earlier transmitted core.

Developmental role

Kanei Uechi is central to understanding how the art was organized and expanded after Kanbun's earlier teaching period.

Curriculum question

The archive distinguishes the earlier three-form core from later kata and pedagogical structures rather than projecting the completed system backward.

Transmission

Postwar Okinawan transmission carried Uechi-Ryū into a wider institutional and international setting.
