Okinawa

A development-history portal for the stage where Uechi-Ryū was organized, expanded, transmitted, and eventually carried internationally.

Systematization

Later Okinawan development added curriculum, teaching structures, and organizational form around the earlier material associated with Kanbun Uechi.

Historical separation

The archive distinguishes what can be attributed to Kanbun's earlier teaching from later Okinawan additions and interpretations.

Lineage continuation

Kanei Uechi and other postwar transmitters are central to understanding how the art continued and changed after Kanbun.

Video evidence

Modern Okinawan demonstrations are useful for documenting current lineage practice when presenter and context are identified.
