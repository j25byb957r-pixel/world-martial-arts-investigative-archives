Sam Chien

A focused portal for the Sam Chien rendering encountered in Southern Chinese martial-arts literature and comparative discussion. The archive treats the name as a research lead rather than assuming that every similarly named form has one identical genealogy.

Current status: terminology and comparative relationship are established research subjects; exact lineage-specific audiovisual examples will be added only after the archive can verify the school, presenter, and form attribution.

Why the separate portal matters

Separating Sam Chien from San Zhan and Sanchin allows the archive to preserve dialect, lineage, regional, and pedagogical differences rather than collapsing related names into a single supposedly identical form.

Audiovisual verification queue

This page deliberately does not reuse a San Zhan or Sanchin video and relabel it Sam Chien. A video belongs here only when its own creator or reliable publishing context identifies it accordingly.
