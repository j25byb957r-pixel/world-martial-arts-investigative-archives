Topic key • process

Transmission

A cross-archive portal for tracking how material moves from person to person, place to place, and generation to generation.

Archive standard: chronology is not genealogy. Similarity can suggest a transmission question, but descent requires an identifiable bridge.

People

Teacher, student, family, school, and organizational relationships are recorded separately according to evidence strength.

Places

Fujian, Wakayama, Okinawa, and later international centers are treated as distinct transmission stages rather than collapsed into one story.

Change

The archive tracks continuity, modification, omission, standardization, convergence, and reinterpretation instead of assuming perfect preservation.

Visual record

Videos can document a modern presentation of a lineage, but they do not independently prove earlier ancestry.
