Topic key • place

Wakayama

A transmission-history portal for the period in mainland Japan where Kanbun Uechi's teaching became publicly organized and where later historical accounts preserve foundational Sanchin practice.

Historical role

Wakayama is the key bridge between Kanbun's Fujian period and the later Okinawan system.

Early training

Retrospective histories describe beginners spending an extended period on Sanchin and moving forward and backward, making directional practice a legitimate historical research question.

Transmission value

Because the Wakayama period is closer to Kanbun's own teaching than later systematization, it helps distinguish inherited material from later curriculum development.
