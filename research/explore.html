Guided exploration

Choose the question, not the conclusion

There is no single required route through this archive. Begin with the kind of question that interests you, then move between history, geography, forms, people, transmission, audiovisual comparison and source criticism.

Archive character: receive information, examine it, compare it, question it, and allow understanding to develop. The interface should help the reader investigate rather than tell the reader what must be believed.
