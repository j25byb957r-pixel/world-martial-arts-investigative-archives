Historical context

What the visual record can and cannot answer

Modern footage can establish how an identified person or lineage presents a form at the time of recording. It cannot by itself tell us that the same choreography existed in Fujian before 1897. For that question the archive prioritizes dated texts, identifiable historical records, provenance, and corroborating testimony.
