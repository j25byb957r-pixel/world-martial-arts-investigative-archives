Evidence boundary

What the visual record can and cannot answer

Modern footage can establish how an identified practitioner, school, or lineage presents a form at the time of recording. It can make structural similarities and differences visible. It cannot by itself date that choreography to an earlier century or prove a direct genealogical bridge to Okinawa. Those questions remain anchored to dated texts, provenance, identifiable historical records, and corroborating testimony.

Comparison discipline: A difference is evidence too. The archive records visible divergence rather than suppressing it to make two traditions appear more closely related.
