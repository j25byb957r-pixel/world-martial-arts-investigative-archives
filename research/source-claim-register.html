Source & Claim Register

Every major historical claim in the archive should be traceable to a source, assigned a confidence level, and separated from interpretation. This register is the beginning of that audit trail.

Confidence system

How entries are graded

Documented: supported by identifiable primary or strong secondary documentation. High confidence: repeated across multiple credible lineage or institutional sources, though not always backed by surviving primary records. Traditional identification: longstanding lineage account that remains historically plausible but incompletely documented. Comparative: similarities or differences observed across traditions without asserting direct descent. Open inquiry: unresolved claim requiring stronger evidence.
