Confidence system

How entries are graded

Documented: supported by identifiable primary or strong secondary documentation. High confidence: repeated across multiple credible lineage or institutional sources, though not always backed by surviving primary records. Traditional identification: longstanding lineage account that remains historically plausible but incompletely documented. Corroborated living transmission: a feature independently observable in surviving lineages or published transmission material, without implying age. Secondary manuscript lead: a potentially important report about older evidence that still requires examination of the underlying document. Comparative: similarities or differences observed across traditions without asserting direct descent. Open inquiry: unresolved claim requiring stronger evidence.
