Michigan Uechi-Ryū: Horton, Keith, and East West Karate

A research file documenting the Michigan transmission line associated with Alan/Allen J. Horton, William L. Keith, Kalamazoo, Grand Rapids, and East West Karate Center. Sources are compared rather than forced into a single narrative when details differ.

LineageDocumented / corroborated

Alan / Allen J. Horton

Multiple Uechi community sources identify Horton as an early Michigan practitioner who trained under Ryuko Tomoyose and began teaching in Michigan in the 1960s. Spelling varies between Alan, Allen, and Allan across surviving sources.

LineageDocumented / corroborated

William L. Keith

Michigan lineage accounts identify William Keith as a student and teaching partner of Horton. Later sources place Keith in Grand Rapids, and a 2003 martial-arts directory lists East West Karate Center at 5330 South Division with William Keith named in the listing.

Dojo historyDocumented with timeline questions

East West Karate Center

Fourth Form Martial Arts preserves a Grand Rapids lineage from William Keith and James Thompson through Donald Joyner, stating that Joyner opened Joyner’s Professional Karate in 1991 and that Jon Olson later renamed the dojo East West Karate Center in 2003. Contemporary 2003 directories also list East West Karate Center and William Keith, which may reflect overlapping instruction, directory lag, ownership transition, or a more complicated naming history. The discrepancy is retained as an open research question.

George Mays

The name George Mays has been requested for inclusion in this regional archive, but the current source search has not yet produced a reliable martial-arts record tying a person of that exact name to Horton, Keith, or East West Karate Center. The name is preserved here as a research lead rather than published as a historical fact. Additional context, spelling, dates, location, rank, or association could resolve the identity.

Working transmission map

Documented regional chain

Ryuko Tomoyose → Alan/Allen J. Horton → William L. Keith → Michigan students and schools. Fourth Form’s historical account further connects Keith and James Thompson to Donald Joyner, and Joyner to the later Grand Rapids East West Karate lineage. This map is a research scaffold, not a claim that every instructor relationship was exclusive or linear.

Source trail

Sources consulted

Fourth Form Martial Arts Studio, “Our History” — preserves a detailed local lineage: Allen Horton as a student of Ryuko Tomoyose, William Keith as Horton’s student, James Thompson’s later role, Donald Joyner’s Grand Rapids school beginning in 1991, and East West Karate Center from 2003 onward.

fourthformgr.com/our-history

Uechi-Ryu.com forum archive, “U.S.-Uechi History” (2004) — Ron Klein identifies Alan J. Horton as an early Michigan Uechi practitioner, says Horton studied under Tomoyose, opened a school in 1965, and names William L. Keith as his student and partner. This is practitioner testimony preserved in a historical forum rather than an institutional record.

uechi-ryu.com/forums — U.S.-Uechi History

2003 martial-arts directory reproductions — list East West Karate Center, 5330 S Division, Grand Rapids, with William Keith named in the business listing. These directory records provide useful contemporaneous corroboration but do not by themselves establish ownership or teaching roles.
